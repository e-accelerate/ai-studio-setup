Operational performance / Personal lab

Getting a large model ready sooner

EnvironmentTwo-box GB10 cluster

FocusTime to request readiness

MeasurementBefore / after

The problem

A large model spread across two machines took about 13 minutes to become ready for its first question. That delay makes restarts and model changes harder to work around.

Engineering focus

The goal was to reduce time until the service could handle its first request. Startup improvements need to be checked alongside output quality and representative inference performance so that a quicker start remains useful.

Reported result

The original portfolio reports approximately 780 seconds before and 170 seconds after the change: about 13 minutes to under 3 minutes. It also states that accuracy was checked afterwards.

Evidence and limits

The original page does not publish the implementation changes, model revision, timing boundaries, repetition count or accuracy evaluation. The figures are approximate reported lab measurements. They do not establish a general speedup for other models or hardware.

Why it matters

Startup time affects recovery and the practical cost of switching models. A deployment should define readiness as a successful application request and document the process needed to recover after a restart.

Source: measurements described in the original eAccelerate portfolio. This is personal lab work; no client engagement or testimonial is implied.
