Connect the workflow

04 / Improve

Measure and hand over

Check performance and output quality, deliver a runbook, and agree on ongoing support.

What delivery should include

Evidence you can inspect.

Scope and acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation. Measurements should describe the workload they actually tested.

  • Quality: representative examples, baseline comparisons and documented failure cases.
  • Performance: hardware, model, precision, prompt size and cold or warm timing conditions.
  • Privacy: agreed data flows and external dependencies, including any downloads or third-party services.
  • Operations: configuration backups, rollback instructions and a runbook for your environment.
